# Figures of the second manuscript

The legends are in `MANUSCRIPT_A.md`, under Figure legends. All seven files are
drawn by `figures/figs_A.py` from the result files, and the numbers behind each
panel are written to `source_data/` under the same name.

| Item | File in `figures/out/` | What it shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Graphical abstract | `FigA0_graphical_abstract.pdf` | one rule, three resources, three answers to a loss |
| Figure 1 | `FigA1_law.pdf` | the model, a grown construct, and the three ways growth stops |
| Figure 2 | `FigA2_axes.pdf` | the factorial, the elasticity in every source, and restart |
| Figure 3 | `FigA3_measurements.pdf` | the model beside published ablations in the mouse |
| Figure 4 | `FigA4_kidney.pdf` | the extension in which progenitors drive branching |
| Figure 5 | `FigA5_predictions.pdf` | predictions, and the effective elasticity that orders the organs |
| Figure S1 | `FigAS1_pairs.pdf` | variation between constructs grown alike and between a person's two kidneys |

`FigB1_after_arrest.pdf`, drawn by the same script, is not a figure of this
manuscript. It holds the panels on recovery after injury, which belong to the
engineering manuscript.

In every panel, filled symbols and solid lines are the model and open symbols
are measurements. Blue, vermillion and green are the liver-like, kidney-like
and pancreas-like sources; purple, orange and black are perfusion, progenitors
and room.
